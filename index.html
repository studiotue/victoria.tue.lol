Act I

Program notes

Hey y’all!

I’m Victoria Black, an actor, dramaturg, and creative producer working across theatre and film. My artistic practice lives at the intersection of curiosity, precision, and play. I’m drawn to characters with texture and contradiction, and I love exploring the rhythms and humanity that make them feel alive onstage and onscreen.

I found my footing in a weekly scene study class at The 4th Wall Acting Studio in Nashville, where I learned how instinct, craft, and imagination could meet in the same room. That foundation eventually led me to the BFA program at the University of Memphis, where I deepened my work in performance, movement, dialects, mask work, dramaturgy, and costume construction.

I’m the Executive Director of Montani StageLab, where I’m committed to building accessible, community centered work and supporting new voices. As I continue growing as an actor and artist, I’m excited to expand my path in arts leadership and the creation of new, thoughtful work.

After work, I’m usually buying and sewing something I probably don’t need, making collages, or writing. Pilates keeps me balanced in and out of the studio, I’m teaching myself Korean and Swedish, and I log an unreasonable number of movies on Letterboxd. <3
